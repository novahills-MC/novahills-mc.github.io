🏙️ NOVAHILLS CITY — THE OFFICIAL PRE-LAUNCH EVENT

🎉 THE OFFICIAL NOVAHILLS CITY PRE-LAUNCH EVENT

Welcome to NovaHills City — a brand-new Minecraft Java Edition 26.2 Creative-to-Survival event where the players build the city themselves.

This is not a pre-built city. The event is about taking an empty, planned capital area and turning it into the permanent NovaHills City through the work of the community.

Game: Minecraft Java Edition 26.2 Event: Creative → Survival Capital: NovaHills City Capital plots: 125 total Core Builders: 100 Housebuilders: 25 City Support Builders: 25 Support Builders are support roles, not additional plots.

The City World is a controlled development world. Players can only build on their allocated plots, plots they purchase from other players, plots they purchase to add to the Capital, or purchased Village Plots, Town Plots, or City Plots.

Resources are gathered in the separate Survival World, accessed through the Survival World portal at the server main spawn, or through a Survival World portal that may be added inside the City World. A server plugin is planned to allow players to easily transfer their resources from the Survival World to their storage in the City World.

🏗️ HOW THE EVENT WORKS

The event begins in Creative so builders can construct the planned capital safely and efficiently.

The city is then transitioned into Survival, becoming the permanent NovaHills City.

The capital build area

  • The capital contains 125 development plots.
  • 100 plots are assigned to Core Builders.
  • 25 plots are Housebuilder plots.
  • City Support Builders do not receive additional plots.
  • Roads and the city layout are prepared.
  • The intended capital build area is flat and build-ready.
  • There is no random water inside the intended city build area.
  • The City World is a controlled development world; it is not a normal Minecraft survival world.
  • Players can only build on their allocated plots, plots they purchase from other players, plots they purchase to add to the Capital, or purchased Village Plots, Town Plots, or City Plots.
  • Resources are gathered in the separate Survival World through the Survival World portal at the server main spawn, or a Survival World portal that may be added inside the City World.
  • A server plugin is planned to allow easy transfer of resources from the Survival World to storage in the City World.
  • Player buildings are built by the players during the event.
  • The Government and Bank are the designated capital facilities, and players will build them during the event.
  • Commands remain available; the world is not locked to Survival during the build event.

🏛️ FOUNDING BUILDER RULES

Government Builder

  • Builds the government building in the capital during the event.
  • Does not keep the capital event plot.
  • The capital keeps the government building and plot.
  • Receives an automatic City Plot.
  • Receives approximately $750,000 loan.
  • Upgrade eligibility: N/A.
  • In-game reward: $225,000.
  • Event Gift Card reward: £20.

Bank Builder

  • Builds the bank in the capital during the event.
  • Does not keep the capital event plot.
  • Receives an automatic Town Plot.
  • Receives approximately $750,000 loan.
  • Upgrades are 50% off.
  • In-game reward: $225,000.
  • Event Gift Card reward: £20.

Major Server Facility Builder

  • Builds the major server facility in the capital.
  • Does not keep the physical capital event plot.
  • May receive 50% of business profits depending on the type of plot.
  • Receives an automatic Village Plot.
  • Receives approximately $750,000 loan.
  • Upgrade eligibility is 50% off if not receiving profits; full price if receiving profits.
  • In-game reward is $225,000 if not receiving profits; $135,000 if receiving profits.
  • Profit share is calculated from net profits after operating expenses, taxes and maintenance costs.

Main Shopping Center Builder

  • Builds the main shopping center in the capital.
  • Does not keep the physical capital event plot.
  • Receives 50% of business profits.
  • Receives an automatic Village Plot.
  • Receives approximately $300,000 loan.
  • Upgrades are full price.
  • In-game reward: $135,000.
  • Profit share is calculated from net profits after operating expenses, taxes and maintenance costs.

Other Founding Builder

  • The capital event building remains their property.
  • Keeps the capital event plot.
  • Receives an automatic Village Plot.
  • Receives approximately $300,000 loan.
  • Loan is repaid through taxes.
  • Upgrades are full price.
  • In-game reward: $135,000–$315,000 depending on plot type.
  • Event Gift Card reward: £5–£20 depending on plot type.

💰 NOVAHILLS ECONOMY & SETTLEMENT SYSTEM

Founding builders are intended to start with a meaningful financial advantage because they helped create the permanent capital.

Normal players can progress through the wider NovaHills economy and eventually purchase their own plots and develop towns and cities.

Normal plot prices

PlotCost
Village Plot$200,000
Town Plot$500,000
City Plot$1,000,000

Upgrades

  • Village → Town: $300,000
  • Town → City: $500,000

City development loans are a revolving development credit facility, rather than a single one-time loan.

A city can request additional development credit when it needs more funding, subject to its current credit limit.

Credit limits can be affected by:

  • Repayment history
  • City revenue
  • Population
  • Business activity
  • Overall development success

Loans continue accumulating during real-world time, including when players are offline.

The standard cycle is:

1 real-world month = 30 real-world days.

🏦 BUSINESS PROFIT SHARING

Where a founding builder receives a percentage of business profits, the calculation is based on:

Gross Revenue − Operating Expenses − Taxes − Maintenance Costs = Net Profit

The builder receives 50% of Net Profit, with NovaHills receiving the remaining 50%.

🏘️ NORMAL PLAYER PROGRESSION

A normal player who did not participate in the founding event does not receive a founding-event capital plot.

They can:

  • Use their allocated House Plot within Capital.
  • Purchase a house or apartment within Capital.
  • Progress through Village, Town and City development.
  • Earn money through the NovaHills economy.
  • Build businesses.
  • Establish and develop settlements elsewhere in the wider NovaHills region.

The purpose is to allow the Capital to remain a finite, prestigious city while giving players a long-term route toward creating their own towns and cities.

🧱 BUILDING RULES

No Copying

Builders must create their own work.

Copying another builder's building, copying tutorials directly, or using another person's build as their own is not allowed.

Architectural inspiration is allowed, but builders are expected to create original work.

Plot Protection

Individual builder plots are protected using WorldGuard / WorldEdit.

Builders must build within their assigned area.

Plot Communication

Builder communication and plot-related information will be handled through the designated event communication channels.

Build Quality

Builds are reviewed during the event.

Staff may review:

  • Build quality
  • Completion
  • Plot compliance
  • Rule compliance
  • Originality
  • Overall suitability for the permanent city

🛡️ ANTI-SWEEP PROTECTION

The event includes Anti-Sweep protection.

The purpose is to prevent builders from taking over or interfering with another builder's assigned work.

Your assigned plot is your responsibility.

Do not build over, modify, grief or interfere with another builder's plot.

📅 DAY 23 — 50% AUDIT

On Day 23, a 50% progress audit takes place.

Builders should have a substantial amount of their assigned work completed by this point.

The audit is used to identify:

  • Builders who are progressing normally
  • Builders who need help
  • Builders who are substantially behind
  • Plots requiring staff attention

The audit is not intended to punish builders who are genuinely progressing.

⚡ EARLY COMPLETION

Builders who finish their assigned work early may be given additional roles or opportunities to help with the wider event.

Early completion does not automatically give a builder another permanent Capital plot.

👥 HELPER RULES

Helpers can assist where permitted, but builders remain responsible for their assigned plot.

Helpers cannot be used to bypass builder selection or plot allocation rules.

🔒 COMMUNICATION & PRIVACY

Official event information will be communicated through the designated NovaHills channels.

Builders should use the official channels for event-related information.

Personal information should not be shared publicly.

Staff decisions and event communications should remain within the appropriate official channels where required.

🏷️ PERMANENT BUILDING CREDITS

Builders who create permanent Capital buildings will receive permanent building credit.

The finished city will retain recognition of the people who contributed to its construction.

Builder credits may form part of the final city showcase and historical records.

🏛️ FOUNDING BUILDER STATUS

Founding Builder recognition is connected to their contribution to the creation of the permanent city.
